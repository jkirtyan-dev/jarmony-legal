Votes express your opinion and for some statements may reveal political, religious or philosophical beliefs. Voting is voluntary and every statement can be skipped. We never publish individual votes: public results only show aggregated numbers, without names or accounts.

We use no ads, tracking or analytics tools, we do not access your location, contacts, photos or microphone, and we do not sell your data.

On your device, sign-in tokens are protected by the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain. Votes not yet sent and the content cache are kept in the app's private storage. The app never stores your password.

3. Processors and transfers

  • Supabase – database, authentication, server functions. Data is stored in the EU, in Paris (AWS eu-west-3).
  • Google – Google sign-in and Firebase Cloud Messaging (notification delivery on both platforms).
  • Apple – Sign in with Apple and the Apple Push Notification service.
  • OpenAI – translating the text of statements to be published into other languages. Only the statement text is sent, without anything identifying its author; OpenAI never receives your votes, e-mail address or account. Request storage is turned off.

Google, Apple and OpenAI may transfer data to the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. On deletion the following is removed immediately:

  • your sign-in account (e-mail address, sign-in methods, sessions);
  • your settings, devices and push tokens;
  • your votes, which are also removed from the totals.

The text of your suggestions and reports is kept detached from your account, without your name, and so are statements already published. We record that a deletion took place under your random user ID (without your e-mail address), so the request is not processed twice and as a security log.

5. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability, object to processing, and withdraw your consent at any time. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account) or request it by e-mail, see the account deletion page. We answer requests within 30 days at the latest.

You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Hungary: NAIH, naih.hu).

6. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Only server-side functions can access the database; the app contains no secret server keys.

7. Age

The app is intended for users aged 16 and over.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will say so in the app or on this page. The effective date is always shown at the top.
