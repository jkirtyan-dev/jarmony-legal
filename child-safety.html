Child Safety Standards

Jarmony – Agree or Disagree (Android and iOS) · Developer: János Kirtyán · Effective: 8 November 2026

Our standard

Jarmony has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any content, suggestion, report or account that sexualises, grooms, exploits or endangers minors is prohibited. This applies to every part of the app and to every user.

How the app limits risk

  • Jarmony is intended for users aged 16 and over.
  • There is no messaging between users, no public profiles, and no photo, video or file uploads.
  • Users can only suggest short text statements. Every suggestion is reviewed by a human moderator and is never published without approval.
  • Votes are only shown as aggregated totals, never linked to a person.

Reporting

You can report a concern in two ways:

  • In the app: on any statement, choose Report, select Offensive or inappropriate and describe the problem.
  • By e-mail: jandalf.dev@gmail.com with the subject “Child safety report”.

Child safety reports are handled with priority, as soon as possible and at the latest within 24 hours.

What we do

  • We remove prohibited content immediately and suspend or delete the account involved.
  • We report apparent CSAM and child exploitation to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the Hungarian police, and preserve the information they need, as required by law.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests related to child safety.

Legal compliance

Jarmony complies with applicable child safety laws, including the laws of Hungary and the European Union.
