Delete your account

Jarmony – Agree or Disagree · János Kirtyán

You can delete your Jarmony account in two ways:

  1. In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. After you confirm, the account is deleted immediately.
  2. By e-mail, if you no longer have access to the app: write to jandalf.dev@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account with the subject “Account deletion”. We verify that the account is yours and delete it within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

  • your sign-in account: e-mail address, sign-in methods, sessions;
  • your settings, devices and push tokens;
  • your votes, which are also removed from the totals.

What is kept

  • the text of your suggestions and reports, detached from your account and without your name, including statements already published;
  • a record that the deletion happened, under your random user ID and without your e-mail address, as a security log.
